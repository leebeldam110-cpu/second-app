# second-app

**London, 1886**: a clean, simple reference plan for drawing Victorian London.

Open `index.html` in a browser. There's no build step; the only external request is Google Fonts.

- Labels appear in tiers as you zoom. Fully zoomed out shows only district names. Zooming in adds landmarks and bridges, then streets and minor places, then stations, piers and everything else. Labels are placed by priority, and any that would overlap are hidden until there's room.
- Lambeth is the focus, and the map opens on it. Westminster, the New Cut and the Chinese quarter at Limehouse and Shadwell are outlined. The rest of London is there for reference.
- Layers can be switched by category:
  - Labels: district names, street names, parks and water.
  - Places, by type: government, churches, theatres and music halls, markets, hospitals/prisons/workhouses, museums and monuments, industry and docks, rookeries and quarters.
  - Transport: bridges (numbered west to east), main-line railways, stations, the Metropolitan and District underground, horse-tram routes (approximate) and steamboat piers.
  - Overlays: focus-area outlines.
- Drag to move. Scroll, pinch or use + / − to zoom. Tap any marker for a note.

Positions come from hand-plotted latitude/longitude. They're good enough for a drawn map, but it isn't a survey.
